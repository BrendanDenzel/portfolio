01

Booking & Payment

Payment is accepted via Venmo, Cash App, cash, or other discussed methods. Full payment is typically due before or after the time of the session but before the products are delivered, unless otherwise discussed in advance.

Deposits are not generally required unless explicitly stated in writing prior to booking for larger or more complex engagements.

Cancellations are accepted at any time. If a session is cancelled with very short notice after travel to the location has already begun, a case-by-case conversation may occur regarding any costs incurred. No automatic penalties apply.

02

Deliverables & Satisfaction

Dizz Studios is committed to delivering quality photos that meet or exceed your expectations. If you are unsatisfied with the results, please communicate this as soon as possible.

  • If you are unhappy with the editing of specific photos, Dizz Studios will re-edit where reasonably possible.
  • If the number of usable photos falls short of expectations due to circumstances within the photographer's control, a discount may be applied at the photographer's discretion.
  • If you are generally dissatisfied with the overall outcome, Dizz Studios will make reasonable efforts to resolve the issue which may include a partial or full refund depending on the situation.
  • For portrait sessions, a reshoot may be offered when circumstances warrant it. A reshoot driven by client preference rather than factors outside the client's control (such as weather or technical failure) may require an additional payment, discussed on a case-by-case basis.
  • For sports sessions, reshoot availability depends on scheduling, location, and the photographer's availability at that time.
Our Approach Refunds and adjustments are handled honestly and directly. Dizz Studios will always act in good faith to make things right.
03

Photo Delivery & Storage

Gallery Access

After your session, photos will be delivered via an online gallery or shared link. You will have access to your photos for a minimum of 30 days from the date of delivery. After this period, photos may be removed from cloud storage without notice.

Backup Storage

Dizz Studios maintains a personal backup of session files on a private hard drive. While no guarantee is made that original files will be retained indefinitely beyond the 30-day window, it is likely that a copy exists on the photographer's personal storage well beyond that point.

If you need photos retrieved after the gallery expires, reach out and every reasonable effort will be made to assist.

Important Dizz Studios is not liable for data loss due to hardware failure or unforeseen technical issues. Clients are strongly encouraged to download and back up their photos promptly after delivery.
04

Copyright & Ownership

Dizz Studios retains full copyright ownership of all photographs taken during any session, regardless of delivery or payment. This is standard practice under U.S. copyright law.

However, clients are granted a broad, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use their photos freely, including:

  • Sharing on social media platforms
  • Sending to friends or family
  • Using for commercial purposes (e.g., advertising clothing, a business, or a product appearing in the photo)
  • Printing for personal or professional use
No Credit Required Clients are never required to credit or tag Dizz Studios when sharing or using their photos. Attribution is appreciated but completely optional.

The only restriction is that photos may not be falsely attributed to the client as their own original photographic work. Claiming you took a photo that was taken by Dizz Studios — whether explicitly or implied through omission in a context where authorship is being claimed is not permitted.

05

Photographer's Use of Photos

Dizz Studios reserves the right to use photos from any session for portfolio, website, and social media purposes, including display on www.dizzstudios.org and associated accounts.

Out of respect for clients, Dizz Studios will always:

  • Reach out before posting any photo that may portray the subject in an unflattering or potentially embarrassing way, and will not post such photos without consent.
  • Immediately remove any photo from public display upon a client's request, for any reason and at any time — no questions asked.
  • Handle photos of minors with additional care, and will not publish any photo of a minor that could be considered revealing, inappropriate, or that captures unintended moments such as clothing malfunctions.
06

Privacy & Data Handling

Dizz Studios does not sell, share, or distribute your photos or personal information to any third party. Your photos are delivered exclusively to you or to the group, team, or organization on whose behalf you booked the session.

The only exceptions are:

  • Photos used by Dizz Studios for its own portfolio or social media, as described in Section 5.
  • Any sharing explicitly authorized by you in writing.
07

AI-Assisted Culling & Sorting

Dizz Studios may use artificial intelligence tools — including Adobe Lightroom's AI-powered features — to assist in the culling (selection), sorting, and organization of photographs from your session. This technology helps identify the best shots efficiently and does not alter the content of your photos in any substantive way.

By booking Dizz Studios, you acknowledge and consent to the use of AI-assisted tools as part of the photo editing and selection workflow.

08

Sessions Involving Minors

When booking a session that will involve minors (anyone under 18 years of age), the person making the booking represents and confirms that they have the appropriate authority to do so — whether as a parent, legal guardian, school representative, coach, or other authorized party.

Dizz Studios shoots solo and all work is conducted professionally and respectfully at all times.

Limitation of Liability

Dizz Studios is not responsible for:

  • Weather or environmental conditions that affect session quality.
  • Technical issues outside the photographer's control (e.g., memory card failure, equipment malfunction).
  • Loss of data after the 30-day delivery window has passed.
  • Circumstances beyond the photographer's reasonable control that prevent a session from occurring.

In any of the above situations, Dizz Studios will work in good faith to find a fair resolution if that may be a reshoot.
